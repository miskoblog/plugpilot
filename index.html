How to Use PlugPilot

SuperPlug AI makes the work. PlugPilot helps with the three decisions around it: what to spend, what to ask for, and what to charge. Start with CreditWise if you've just bought, Briefed if you're about to make something, or Priced if you're selling to clients.

Before you start

Everything runs in your browser. There is nothing to connect, no keys to paste and no account to create. Each tool follows the same pattern: fill in the form on the left, read the result on the right, press Copy to paste it somewhere, or press Save to keep it. Saved runs appear under the result. Open puts a saved run's inputs back in the form so you can change them and regenerate; Delete removes it.

Figures marked est. are placeholders, not SuperPlug's real prices. Whenever SuperPlug shows you a real credit cost in its plan, type that number over the estimate.

1 CreditWise — know what a month costs

Your plan includes a fixed number of credits and connections each month. CreditWise helps you plan them, set sensible limits and track them.

Monthly Credit Budget Planner

  1. Check Monthly creation credits and Monthly MCP connections match your plan.
  2. Set Connections used per job — roughly how many back-and-forth requests a job takes.
  3. In the table, enter How many of each job you expect this month. Overwrite Credits per job with the real cost when you know it.
  4. The result shows whether the month fits, how much is left, and how many more of each job would still fit. Press Save to keep the plan.

Spending Guardrail Calculator

  1. Press Use Planner total to pull in your plan, or type Planned job credits yourself.
  2. Set the Revision rounds per job and Cost of one revision (% of original) so changes don't eat the month.
  3. Optionally split the month across up to four clients in the client rows.
  4. The result gives a recommended daily cap, an "ask me first" threshold and a per-client budget to enter in SuperPlug's own settings. Save keeps it.

Credit Ledger & Burn-Down

  1. Set Credits this month, Cycle starts and Cycle length (days).
  2. Every time you approve a job in SuperPlug, enter the job, its credits, date and client and press Add entry. Use Remove to correct a mistake.
  3. The result shows credits used, your daily pace and the day you'd run out. Save stores a snapshot of the whole month; Open brings it back.

2 Briefed — walk into the chat prepared

SuperPlug's AI asks you questions and then makes the work. Briefed gets your answers ready first.

One-Sentence Brief Builder

  1. Pick the Job, then fill in Business name, What the business is, Goal or offer and Audience.
  2. Choose a Tone and which Your AI you use. If you've filled in a brand in the next tool, pick it under Brand kit.
  3. Tick everything under Assets I have ready. The result lists what's still missing and gives a completeness score.
  4. Press Copy, paste the brief into your AI chat, and attach the files. Save keeps the brief.

Brand Kit Prep Sheet

  1. Choose a Brand slot (up to three, matching Premium's three brand kits).
  2. Fill in Brand name, the three color fields, Fonts, Tone words, Products or services, Approved claims and Never say.
  3. Copy the finished block into SuperPlug when it asks about your brand. Save keeps a named copy.

Revision Request Sharpener

  1. Choose the Asset type, then type one change request per line under Feedback (one request per line).
  2. Add anything that must not change in Keep untouched.
  3. Each line is matched to the part of the asset it's about, with a risk label for whether it could trigger a full regeneration. Copy gives you the cleaned-up message to send; Save keeps it.

3 Priced — know what to charge

Your commercial license lets you sell what you make. Priced turns market rates into quotes you can defend.

Deliverable Rate Calculator

  1. Pick the Deliverable and Quantity, then Market size, Scope and Turnaround.
  2. Adjust Market low and Market high to what clients near you actually pay.
  3. Enter Cost per credit ($) and Your hourly rate ($) to see your margin at a floor, target and premium price.
  4. Press Add to price sheet to send the target price to the third tool, or Save to keep the quote.

Monthly Retainer Package Builder

  1. Set the price of each package (Starter, Growth, Premium) and what a post, a short video and a report cost you in credits and time.
  2. Enter Clients you want to serve to see whether your monthly credits can carry them.
  3. The result shows margin per package and how many clients each package fits. Save keeps it.

One-Page Price Sheet

  1. Fill in Your business name, Tagline, Contact details and Terms.
  2. Press Import from Rate Calculator to bring in quotes you sent over, or add items by hand with Add item. Tick Include retainer packages to add your packages.
  3. Press Print for a clean one-page sheet or PDF, Copy for plain text, or Save to keep it.

One thing to know

Everything you enter and save lives only in this browser on this device. There are no accounts and nothing syncs to other devices. If you clear your browser's site data, your saved work goes with it, so use Copy for anything you can't afford to lose.
